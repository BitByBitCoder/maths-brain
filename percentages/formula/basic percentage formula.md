The formula I used is the basic **percentage formula**:

Percentage = (Part / Whole) × 100

This formula answers questions like:

- What percent of 80 is 20?
    
- What percent of ( \frac{2}{7} ) is ( \frac{1}{35} )?
    
- 30 is what percent of 120?
    

### Example 1

What percent of 80 is 20?

[  
\frac{20}{80}\times100  
]

[  
25%  
]

### Example 2

What percent of (\frac{2}{7}) is (\frac{1}{35})?

[  
\frac{\frac{1}{35}}{\frac{2}{7}}\times100  
]

[  
10%  
]

### How to identify "Part" and "Whole"

In a question of the form:

> **What percent of A is B?**

- **Whole = A**
    
- **Part = B**
    

So:

# [  
\text{Percentage}

\frac{B}{A}  
\times100  
]

For your question:

> What percentage of (\frac{2}{7}) is (\frac{1}{35})?

- Whole = (\frac{2}{7})
    
- Part = (\frac{1}{35})
    

Therefore:

[  
\frac{\frac{1}{35}}{\frac{2}{7}}\times100  
]

That's why we used that formula. This is one of the most important percentage formulas for LDC aptitude questions.
